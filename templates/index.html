Mastering the Pomodoro Method 🍅

Simple steps, brain hacks, and the science of deep focus.
🎯 The 4-Step Gameplan
1 🎯

Pick One Target

Type your focus topic in the input bar. Single-tasking only—no multitasking allowed!

2 ⏱️

25-Min Sprint

Hit Start. Dive deep until the gentle singing bowl chime signals time's up.

5-Min Reset

Step away! Stretch, grab water, or rest your eyes. Don't scroll social media.

4 🌴

15-Min Long Break

After 4 focus cycles, enjoy a longer recharge to reset your mental battery.

🧠 Brain Science Fun Facts
🍅

Why "Pomodoro"?

Francesco Cirillo invented this in the late 1980s as a university student using a literal tomato-shaped kitchen mechanical timer (pomodoro is Italian for tomato!).

⚡

Beat Parkinson’s Law

"Work expands to fill the time available for its completion." Giving yourself a strict 25-minute limit tricks your brain into immediate action and crushes procrastination.

🧠

Default Mode Network (DMN)

Your brain actually consolidates memory and solves complex bugs while you're daydreaming on your 5-minute break, not while actively staring at the problem.

👁️

The 20-20-20 Rule

Every break, gaze at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent eye muscle spasms and visual fatigue.

🏆

Fair Metric: 1 🍅 = 25 Mins

Tomato badges and stats are standardized to 25 minutes of cumulative focus. Whether you customize session durations or sprint in blocks, achievements stay fair and consistent!

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Transparent, cozy, and respect-first data practices.

At PomoHaven, your focus space is sacred. We believe productivity tools should be distraction-free, respectful of your privacy, and completely transparent about data handling.

🔐

1. Data Collection & Authentication

When you sign in with Google or create an account, Google profile info (name, email, avatar) is used solely to authenticate your account and associate study streaks, completed focus sessions, and custom preferences across your devices. Guest mode requires zero personal data and preserves your session history locally.

📊

2. Usage Analytics

Anonymous usage metrics and aggregated interactions are collected via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us identify performance bottlenecks, understand general feature adoption, and continuously improve user experience.

🛡️

3. Data Control & Ownership

Your data is never sold or shared with third-party advertisers. You maintain complete control: you can clear your study logs anytime using Clear History, export your history as CSV, or request full account and data deletion anytime via the built-in Feedback tool.
