Mastering the Pomodoro Method 🍅

Simple steps, brain hacks, and the science of deep focus.
🎯 The 4-Step Gameplan
1 🎯

Pick One Target

Type your focus topic in the input bar. Single-tasking only—no multitasking allowed!

2 ⏱️

25-Min Sprint

Hit Start. Dive deep until the gentle singing bowl chime signals time's up.

3 ☕

5-Min Reset

Step away! Stretch, grab water, or rest your eyes. Don't scroll social media.

4 🌴

15-Min Long Break

After 4 focus cycles, enjoy a longer recharge to reset your mental battery.

🧠 Brain Science Fun Facts
🍅

Why "Pomodoro"?

Francesco Cirillo invented this in the late 1980s as a university student using a literal tomato-shaped kitchen mechanical timer (pomodoro is Italian for tomato!).

⚡

Beat Parkinson’s Law

"Work expands to fill the time available for its completion." Giving yourself a strict 25-minute limit tricks your brain into immediate action and crushes procrastination.

🧠

Default Mode Network (DMN)

Your brain actually consolidates memory and solves complex bugs while you're daydreaming on your 5-minute break, not while actively staring at the problem.

👁️

The 20-20-20 Rule

Every break, gaze at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent eye muscle spasms and visual fatigue.
